# Alien Puzzle Garden Program

## Current Research Taste

Prefer patterns that:
- survive rule removal
- show compression behavior
- produce naming debt that can be split
- create interpretable survival differences under mutation pressure

Avoid:
- purely score-attached patterns
- single-rule artifacts that vanish without leaving a useful diagnosis
- claims of proof, novelty, or mathematical importance before mutation tests

## Evolution Policy

- Promote robust candidates only as local parent signals.
- Split fragile broad names into smaller testable names.
- Prefer mutations that clarify why a pattern survived, weakened, or broke.
- Keep the experiment loop cheap, deterministic, and inspectable before adding paid APIs.
